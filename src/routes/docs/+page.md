title: "Introduction"
description: "An introduction to Customer Actions for Jira: how the app extends your Jira Service Management customer portal with configurable actions."
---

Customer Actions for Jira helps you make your customer portal more interactive.

By enabling portal users to edit issues, add additional information, or link related issues, you can greatly extend the capabilities of your customer portal.

Learn more about how to get started and set up advanced actions in our product documentation.
