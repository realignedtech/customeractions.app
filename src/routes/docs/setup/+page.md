---
title: "Setup"
description: "How to install Customer Actions for Jira from the Atlassian Marketplace and find the configuration in your service project."
---

In order to install, perform the following steps:

- Log into your Jira instance as an administrator
- Click the cog icon in the top right-hand corner and choose "Apps"
- Search for "Customer Actions" and click the app
- Click "Get App" and "Get it now"
- Wait until the app is installed. You should now find the configuration UI in every Jira Service Management project.
